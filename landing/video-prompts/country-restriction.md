# Country Restriction — 3-Part Video Prompt (30s total)

**Style:** Talking head (center-right) with screen overlays appearing one by one. Man speaks directly to camera. Clean transitions, no jump cuts. Screen inserts animate in (slide-up + fade, 0.4s ease).

**Tone:** Confident, clear, benefit-first. Dark background matching brand gradient (`#0f172a` → `#1e1b4b`). Speaker well-lit, background slightly blurred.

**Audio:** Voiceover-quality recording. Background: subtle low hum, no music competing with speech.

---

## Part 1 — Problem + Introduction (0:00 – 0:10)

### Audio Script

> "Doing business globally means welcoming visitors from anywhere — but not every country should have access to your site. Maybe you are licensing content by region, blocking spam traffic from certain locations, or running a beta launch in specific markets. WPEPP Country Restriction lets you block or allow entire countries with one toggle. No CDN configs. No firewall rules. No code."

**Delivery:** Begin with direct eye contact. Gesture open palms on "welcoming visitors". Small head shake on "not every country". Firm nod on "No CDN configs."

### Audio Script

> "Just turn it on. Choose Block list to hide your site from selected countries — or Allow list to show it only to specific countries. Then start typing any country name. Free users get 2 countries. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited. Customize your blocked page with your own title and message so visitors know why they cannot access your site."

**Delivery:** Hand gesture mimicking a toggle switch on "turn it on". Point left on "Block list", point right on "Allow list". Typing gesture on "start typing". Hold up two fingers on "2 countries."

## Production Notes

| Aspect | Spec |
|--------|------|
| **Total duration** | 30 seconds (3 × 10s) |
| **Aspect ratio** | 16:9 (1920×1080) |
| **Speaker** | Man, 30-45, professional but approachable. Dark collared shirt or blazer. Well-lit with key+fill. |
| **Background** | Dark gradient matching brand (`#0f172a` → `#1e1b4b`), subtle vignette. Speaker area slightly blurred (depth of field effect). |
| **Screen recordings** | 1920×1080 capture of WPEPP admin → Site Access → Country Restriction. Mouse cursor hidden. Clean, no bookmarks bar. |
| **Animations** | All screen inserts: slide-up + fade (0.4s, ease-out). Badges: scale-bounce (0.4s). All timed to match script cadence. |
| **Transitions** | Part to part: 0.3s crossfade. Within part: no cuts, elements animate in/out. |
| **Captions** | Burn-in captions bottom-center, white text with dark drop shadow. Highlight key words in accent color (`#e11d48`). |
| **Audio levels** | Voice: -6dB peak. Background: -24dB. No music. |
